Always translate, except…

Pick the language you want to read in and add the ones you already know. Every other page translates by itself as it opens. No buttons, no copy-paste.

Reply in their language

Type in your own language in any text box on a foreign site. A small button appears inside the box: click it, or press ⌥⇧T (Alt+Shift+T), and your text is converted to the site's language. ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z) undoes it.

See the original anytime

Hold ⌥ (Alt) and hover any paragraph to see what it originally said, or switch the whole page back from the toolbar.

Pages keep working

Text is translated in place, so links, buttons, menus and modern web apps keep working. Visible text goes first, the rest as you scroll.

Your rules

Skip any site with one switch. “Translate anyway” when you want an exception. Your typing is never translated unless you ask.

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-up, no ads, no analytics. Text is sent only to Google Translate to be translated. Privacy policy
