Auto Translate Privacy Policy

Auto Translate sends the text it translates to the translation service you use (Google Translate unless you choose another), and nothing else to anyone. I don't run any servers and I don't collect anything about you.

Applies to the Auto Translate extension for Chrome · Last updated: November 14, 2026

Data I collect

None. Auto Translate has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking code. It never sends anything to me.

What is sent for translation

To translate, the extension sends text to a translation service and puts the result back on the page:

  • Web page text. When a page is in a language you don't read, the visible text of that page (and labels such as placeholders, tooltips and image descriptions) is sent for translation. Pages in your own languages, sites you've marked “Never translate”, and all pages while automatic translation is off are not sent.
  • Text you type, only when you ask. Text in a text box is sent only when you press the convert button or its keyboard shortcut, or press Enter in the search box of a site in a language you don't read (if “Search in your language” is on). Password fields are ignored.
  • Text you select, only when you ask. Selected text is sent only when you click the Translate pill.

Which service receives it

  • Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com), the default, under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Cloud Translation API (translation.googleapis.com), if you add your own Google Cloud key, under your Google Cloud account.
  • An AI service you choose (OpenAI-compatible or Anthropic-compatible), if you enter its address and key in Settings. Text goes to the address you entered, under that service's own privacy policy and terms. If the setting “If it fails, use free Google Translate instead” is on (it is by default), text that service fails to translate is sent to Google Translate instead; turn it off to keep everything with your service.
  • Chrome's on-device translator, if you turn on offline translation and the online service can't be reached. This happens entirely on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

The extension sends only the text to be translated, the languages involved and, for an AI service, short instructions on how to translate. It does not send your browsing history, the addresses of the pages you visit, or any information about you.

Exchange rates

If “Show prices and units in yours” is on, the extension downloads public exchange rates about once a day from open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API). That request contains no page content and nothing about you.

Stored in your browser

  • Your settings: your languages, whether the text-box button is on, and the list of sites you chose never to translate. These are kept in Chrome's storage and, if you use Chrome sync, follow your Google account to your other computers.
  • Your API keys (Google Cloud or an AI service), if you add them. They are kept only on this computer and only sent to the service they belong to, with translation requests.
  • The words you chose never to translate, with your settings.
  • Exchange rates, cached for a day.
  • Recent translations are kept in memory so the same text isn't translated twice. They are discarded when Chrome closes the extension's background process.

Remove the extension to delete everything it stored.

Permissions

Read and change data on websites is needed to find a page's text, replace it with the translation, and show the convert button inside text boxes. Storage keeps your settings. Access to translate.googleapis.com and translation.googleapis.com is used only to request translations. If you set up an AI service, Chrome asks you to allow access to that one address, and it is used only to request translations.

How data is used

Text is used only to translate it for you. It is not sold, not used for advertising or credit decisions, and not shared with anyone other than the translation service you use, for the translation itself. The use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Auto Translate doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
