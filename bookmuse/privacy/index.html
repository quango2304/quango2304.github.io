Bookmuse Privacy Policy

You can read in Bookmuse without an account, and then your library stays on your device. If you sign in (with Apple, Google or an email and password), we store your library on our servers in Japan so it syncs between your devices. Online features send only what they need. We don't show ads, sell your data or track you across other apps and websites. You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

Applies to Bookmuse · Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Also at bookmuse.wanngo.com/privacy (with Vietnamese and Spanish) · Delete your account: bookmuse.wanngo.com/delete-account

In short: without an account, your library stays on your device. With one, it is stored on our servers in Japan so it can sync. Online features send only what they need. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold. You can delete your account at any time.

Who we are

Bookmuse is made by Quan Ngo, an independent developer ("we"). This policy covers the Bookmuse apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, bookmuse.wanngo.com and the web dashboard bookmuse-app.wanngo.com.

What we keep

Signing in is optional. Without an account, your books, words, highlights, progress and settings stay on your device and we receive none of them. With an account we keep:

  • Sign-in: an account ID, your email (or Apple's private relay address) and name from Apple or Google, and your Google photo, copied once if you have no picture. With email sign-in, your password as a one-way hash (argon2id). Sign-in codes go out through Oracle's email service.
  • Your library: book details and reading positions, highlights, notes, bookmarks, saved words and review progress, lookup history, Ask AI conversations, reading time, folders, reminders, settings, catalog addresses (never catalog passwords) and a random ID for each installation.
  • Book files and covers: in private storage, reached only through short-lived signed links. Identical files may share one stored copy; no one can see who else has it.
  • Sessions and usage: device type, app version and last use of each sign-in, tokens as hashes, and counts of limited features for the free plan.

Where it is stored

On Oracle Cloud in Japan (Osaka), reached through Cloudflare. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. If you live elsewhere, your data is transferred to Japan. We keep no database backups, so deleted data can't be restored.

What you make public

  • Share links work for 7 days. Anyone with the link sees the book's cover, title, author, file size and your display name, and anyone signed in can add a copy, which stays theirs. Highlights and notes are never shared.
  • The feed is public, even to people who aren't signed in: your posts (book, text, rating, quote, share link), comments, likes and profile (name, picture, join date, post count, followers and following). We keep your reports and blocks. A report hides an item from you at once and from everyone after several reports. We may remove content and stop an account from using the feed. Deleted posts and comments leave the feed at once; their text stays hidden until you delete your account.

BookMuse AI

BookMuse AI needs an account. When you use it, our server receives the text you look up, your questions and the parts of the book needed to answer (such as the table of contents, the page on screen and passages it searches), and passes them to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the answer under their terms. Anthropic gets a scrambled form of your account ID, never your name or email. We don't store or log this content, and we don't use it to train AI models. Answers you keep sync with your account. Without an account, or when BookMuse AI is unavailable, lookups use on-device translation.

If you report an answer, we receive that answer, the question before it, the model, the chat, the book's title, your reason and note, the time and the app version, linked to your account if you're signed in. We use reports to improve the AI and keep it safe.

AI agents

If you connect an AI agent (such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor), it can read, add, change and delete your books, words, highlights, notes and folders. What it reads goes to that company under its own policy. Agents can't delete your account, erase your data or use BookMuse AI. We keep the agent's name and session until you disconnect it.

Google Drive and Dropbox

You can connect these to import books. The connection is kept in your account, with its token encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Files download straight to your device, not through our server. We use this access only to import the books you choose. Disconnect in Settings › Connections, or delete your account, and we revoke it.

  • Google Drive: only the drive.file permission, so Bookmuse can open only the files you pick, plus that account's email. It can't see the rest of your Drive.
  • Dropbox: Bookmuse reads your account email and your folder and file names, and downloads the EPUB files you choose. It can't change or delete anything.

Bookmuse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use Google user data for advertising, don't sell it, don't use it to train AI models, and no person reads it unless you ask us to (for example in a support request), it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Other services

These receive your IP address, like any internet request, and work under their own privacy policies.

  • Open Library: when you search for a book to post about, your device sends what you type to Open Library (Internet Archive). We don't see your searches. Our server downloads the cover you pick.
  • Book catalogs: Project Gutenberg and servers you add (Calibre, Komga, Stump) are reached directly from the app. A catalog's password goes only to that server.
  • Downloads: voices from our public GitHub release, translation languages from Apple or Google.
  • Translation on Android: Google's ML Kit runs on your device and your text stays there. It sends Google diagnostics, with or without an account: device model, Android version, app version, timings, error codes, event types, languages and an installation ID (Google's disclosure). On Apple devices, Apple Translation runs on your device under Apple's terms.

Website and logs

bookmuse.wanngo.com has no analytics, ads or tracking cookies; your browser remembers your light or dark choice. The dashboard uses only the cookies and browser storage it needs to work. Apple's and Google's sign-in buttons load from them, and Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie.

Server logs hold the time, address path, result, duration, app platform and version and your account ID, never your books, notes, words, AI content, passwords or tokens, and are kept only briefly. IP addresses deliver traffic and limit abuse; we don't store them with your account.

What we don't do

No ads, no advertising ID, no selling or renting of data, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. The apps have no analytics or crash-reporting tools, apart from ML Kit's diagnostics on Android.

Who receives data

Only those who run parts of Bookmuse, each for its part: Oracle (servers, storage, email), Cloudflare (delivery and protection), Anthropic (BookMuse AI), Apple and Google (sign-in, and ML Kit on Android), Open Library, and Google Drive or Dropbox if you connect them. We may disclose data if the law requires it. If Bookmuse is ever transferred, this policy keeps applying to the data collected under it.

How long we keep it

  • Account and library: until you delete them.
  • Sign-in codes: a day. Ended sessions: about a week.
  • Lookup history: 30 days. AI usage counts: 40 days. Reported AI answers: 180 days, or until you delete your account.
  • Items you delete disappear from your devices at once. Their last text stays hidden in your account until you use Erase All Data or delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete Account (Settings › Account) deletes your account and everything in it right away. See what is deleted and how to ask by email. Deleting your Apple Account does the same.
  • Erase All Data (Settings) clears your library but keeps the account, profile, feed posts, reported answers and Drive or Dropbox connections.
  • You can see, correct and download your data in the app and on the dashboard, and export highlights (Markdown) and words (CSV).

You can also email us for a copy, correction or deletion, or to object to how we use your data; we answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), these are legal rights and you can complain to your data protection authority. Our legal bases are providing the service you asked for, our legitimate interest in keeping Bookmuse secure and the feed safe, and your consent for optional connections, which you can withdraw by disconnecting them.

Children

Bookmuse is for people aged 13 and over (or older, where your country's minimum age is higher). If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Bookmuse 1.3 and earlier

These versions had no account: your library stayed on your device and your own iCloud, which we can't access, and BookMuse AI sent lookups from your device to OpenRouter. Updating starts with an empty library; nothing moves from iCloud to our servers.
