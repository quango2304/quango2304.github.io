Privacy Policy

My apps are built to keep your data yours. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Applies to all apps published by quango2304 · Last updated: September 24, 2026

Data I collect

None. I don't run servers that receive your data, and my apps contain no analytics, advertising or tracking code. I can't see what you store in my apps, and I never track you across other companies' apps or websites. You never need an account or a login.

Where your data is stored

Everything you create in my apps — for example saved items, notes, history, progress and settings — is stored on your device. If you use iCloud, it syncs between your devices through your own private iCloud storage, which Apple operates under Apple's Privacy Policy. I have no access to it. Passwords and API keys you enter are kept in the Keychain and sync only through iCloud Keychain.

Services you choose to connect

Some apps let you connect a third-party service with your own account or API key — for example an AI provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter, or a server you run yourself. This is always optional and off until you set it up, and the app tells you what will be sent before you do.

When you connect one, the content needed for the feature you use (for example, the text you ask to translate or explain, and your questions about it) is sent directly from your device to that service. That exchange is between you and the service, under your account and its own terms and privacy policy — it is not sent to me, and I don't share it with anyone. Please review the service's privacy policy before connecting it.

Features provided by Apple, such as Apple Translation and Apple Intelligence, run on your device under Apple's privacy terms.

Permissions

Apps only ask for the permissions a feature needs, and only when you use it — for example notifications for reminders you schedule, or local network access to reach a server on your own network. You can change these at any time in your device's Settings.

Your choices and deletion

  • Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual items in the app, or use the app's erase option in its Settings to delete everything on all your devices.
  • To stop sending data to a service you connected (withdraw consent), switch off or disconnect it in the app's Settings and remove your key.
  • Deleting an app removes its data from that device. Data synced to iCloud can be managed in your device's iCloud settings.

Children

My apps don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: quango2304@gmail.com.
