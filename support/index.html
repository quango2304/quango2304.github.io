To help me fix a problem quickly, please include:

  • The app name and version (in the app's Settings → About)
  • Your device and iOS version
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened
  • A screenshot, if you can

Common questions

My data isn't syncing

Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for the app. Sync can take a few minutes.

How do I delete my data?

Use the erase option in the app's Settings, or see the privacy policy.

A connected service (such as an AI provider) isn't working

Check that your API key is valid and your account with that service has credit, then try again. You can switch back to the built-in Apple features at any time.
